## Long description

**HAREKÂT** is a Turkish-military-themed **first-person squad battle royale**. Drop in as a ten-soldier team, maintain your chain of command, and be the last squad standing as the operation boundary closes.

This is not a war documentary. It is a **fictional field exercise**. Sides are named only **Blue force** and **Red force**. No real-world organizations or groups are named.

### Squad & command

- **10-soldier teams:** Team Leader, Marksman, Machine Gunner, Medic, Radioman, Grenadier, and Riflemen.
- **Ranks & chain of command:** When the commander falls, authority passes to the next senior rank — the squad keeps fighting.
- **Role-driven loadouts:** Coordination beats lone-wolf heroics.

### Insertion

- Insert by **T-70** helicopter or **Kirpi** armored vehicle.
- Choosing your insertion sector is the first decisive call of the match.

### Weapons & gear

Turkish-produced weapon catalog: **SAR 9**, **Canik TP9**, **SAR 109T**, **MPT-55**, **MPT-76**, **G3A7**, **KNT-76**, **JNG-90 Bora-12**, **PMT-76**, **Escort** shotgun, and more. Manage armor, helmets, medical items, and boosts.

### Operation area

- Map: **Kuzgun Vadisi** — mountains, valley floor, villages, outpost, and forward operating points.
- Shrinking **operation boundary** (zone) phases raise pressure.
- Call **artillery support** for tactical advantage (rules and role dependent).

### What we promise

- Cosmetics-focused progression — **no pay-to-win**.
- Offline: play as commander with AI teammates and rival squads.
- Online-ready architecture in progress — see the Early Access roadmap.

**One order. Ten soldiers. One valley. Last squad standing.**
